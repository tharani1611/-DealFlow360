# DealFlow360 — Hackathon Demo Script (5-Minute Walkthrough)

This script provides a structured, presentation-friendly 5-minute walkthrough of **DealFlow360** for live demonstrations, hackathon presentations, and stakeholder reviews.

---

## 0:00 — Login & Visual Identity (Neo Glass System)
- **Presenter**: "Welcome to DealFlow360 — an intelligent, multi-tenant CRM designed for sales teams."
- **Action**: Log in with demo credentials (`demo@dealflow360.com` / `Demo123!`).
- **Highlight**: Notice the unified **Neo Glass Design System** combining glassmorphic translucency with high-contrast neo-brutalist telemetry panels.

---

## 0:20 — Operational Command Center (Executive Dashboard)
- **Presenter**: "Upon logging in, executives and sales leads immediately see real-time pipeline telemetry rather than generic charts."
- **Highlight**:
  - **Open Pipeline & Weighted Forecast**: Backend-authoritative weighted calculation based on deal values and win probabilities (`CRM-derived estimate`).
  - **Pipeline Concentration Risk Warning**: Flags if top deals account for > 50% of total pipeline value.
  - **Pipeline Stage Distribution**: Stage-by-stage visualizer showing total and weighted deal values.
  - **Executive Sales Attention Center**: Lists prioritized operational items requiring immediate action.

---

## 1:00 — Deal Health Telemetry & Risk Detection
- **Presenter**: "Let's inspect a deal requiring health recovery."
- **Action**: Click on **DEAL-000001 (Acme Digital Transformation Contract)**.
- **Highlight**:
  - **Executive Snapshot Bar**: Displays health score (e.g. `68/100 STABLE`), expected close date, primary risk factors, and overdue touchpoints.
  - **Deterministic Risk Engine**: Highlights exact evaluated risk factors (`OVERDUE_ACTIVITY`, `CLOSING_SOON_LOW_STAGE`).
  - **AI Trust Fact Panel**: Answers "Why this score?" using backend-computed metrics without hallucinatory assumptions.

---

## 1:45 — Smart Next Best Action & Execution
- **Presenter**: "DealFlow360 does not stop at insights — it connects telemetry directly to sales execution."
- **Action**: Inspect the **AI Next Best Action** recommendation. Click **Log Activity / Execute Recommendation**.
- **Highlight**: Automatically pre-fills activity form (type, title, description, priority) so sales reps can execute follow-up tasks in 1 click.

---

## 2:15 — Relationship Intelligence & Account Briefings
- **Presenter**: "Now let's switch to the customer relationship view."
- **Action**: Navigate to **Customers** ➔ Select **Acme Industries**.
- **Highlight**:
  - **Relationship Intelligence Telemetry**: Shows engagement score (`74/100 ENGAGED`), open deal values, and score breakdown.
  - **Sales Briefing Drawer**: Open **✦ Sales Briefing**. Displays executive account overview, key talking points, suggested next steps, and a pre-drafted follow-up email with one-click clipboard copy.

---

## 3:00 — AI Command Center & Security Guardrails
- **Presenter**: "Finally, our AI Command Center allows natural-language queries over your CRM telemetry."
- **Action**: Navigate to **AI Intelligence** ➔ Run preset query: *"Which deals need immediate follow-up attention?"*
- **Highlight**:
  - **CRM Facts vs AI Interpretation**: Shows exact context count analyzed.
  - **Enterprise Security Guardrails**: Strict tenant isolation, prompt injection boundary tags, and 100% read-only non-mutating guarantee.

---

## 3:45 — Closing Summary
- **Presenter**: "DealFlow360 transforms raw CRM data into transparent, explainable sales actions — giving sales teams complete pipeline clarity."
